Getting reminded

Jot can buzz your phone itself. Turn on Notifications in Settings once, and every note with Remind me set pings you at that time, even with Jot closed. (This needs Jot's notification helper running where Jot is hosted. If Settings says it's not available, use the Calendar way below.)

  1. Open Jot from its home-screen icon (not Safari).
  2. Settings → Notifications → tap Allow when your iPhone asks.
  3. That's it. Tap a notification and Jot opens that note.

The time comes from Remind me on the note — at the time, an hour before, a day before. Only the time is sent to Jot's helper; the words stay on your phone. A reminder can land up to a minute late, and if your phone is offline it arrives when you're back online.

  1. Open any reminder and tap Remind me on my phone (it says Also add to Calendar once notifications are on).
  2. Your phone asks where to put it — choose Add to Calendar. It alerts you like anything else in there.
